# os-48df10a2 — Phase 13 item 4: federation read remotes, cross-repo request ingress, and the request.* rows through which mirror edits and dashboard actions enter

Build plan Phase 13 item 4: federation read remotes and cross-repo
request ingress (III.N), and the `request.*` rows through which mirror
edits and dashboard actions enter the ledger, closing the injection
corpus's mirror arm
(III.J row 2, routed here by the Phase 9 exit record). Charter §II.15
("Federation is projection-only: one ledger per repository, org-level
views read across ledgers (remotes addressed uniformly), cross-repo
work enters as request events into the target ledger; there is no
super-ledger, because there is no super-authority"), III.N row 4
("Federation is projection-only: uniform read remotes, request-event
ingress, no cross-ledger write path — proven by its absence"), III.J
row 2's mirror arm ("embedded instructions in intents, mirrors, and
tool output are quoted as data, never obeyed").

## What the tree actually shows

- **`request.*` is a name with no rows.** `protocol.md` catalogs it
  ("inbound proposals from projection surfaces: mirror edits,
  dashboard actions"); `transitions.json` has no row, the affordance
  catalog no entry, the keyring no capability, so the injection suite
  cannot speak about it and `lanes.md` records III.J row 2 as
  two-thirds met with the mirror arm routed here.
- **The suite's method is settled.** It does not test that hostile
  text is disbelieved (no model under `next/`); it asserts that
  believing the text changes nothing: the dispatcher's reachable set
  is derived from the boundary and pinned, the corpus is planted in
  payloads and subjects, and the situation read is swept for the
  marker (`obligations.md`: a message carries no body into the read;
  the subject is carried only when it resolves to a contract).
- **Reading a remote ledger is one client.** `internal/gitref` fetches
  a ledger ref and materializes it locally; the remote posture's
  session verifies from genesis under the chain's own keyring; the
  projections rebuild byte-identically from any verified chain; the
  report is one ledger's view.
- **`message.sent` is the standing-only relay**: any enrolled active
  actor appends it, it grants nothing, its `to` is read leniently by
  the projection, and its body is read only by the deliberate second
  act. A request is the same kind of thing with a typed shape and a
  reader obliged to answer.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

**D1. Two verbs, at `seed/6`.** `request.filed` is the one ingress
verb: a fact, changing no state, strict
`{origin, kind, reference, summary, about?}` — `origin` the surface
the proposal came from (a mirror or dashboard name, or a federation
remote's name), `kind` one of `mirror-edit`, `dashboard-action`,
`cross-repo`, `reference` a commit-anchored ref or an artifact digest
naming what was proposed (the mirror edit's content is an artifact or
a ref into the mirror's own history, never a body in the payload),
`summary` ≤ 200 bytes, `about` a contract id when the proposal
concerns one. The subject is the contract when `about` resolves to
one on the chain, else `system`; a subject that resolves to nothing
refuses. `request.answered` is the dispatcher's close:
`{request, outcome, intent?, reason?}`, `outcome` one of `filed`
(with `intent` the position of the `intent.filed` the dispatcher
appended for it) or `declined` (with `reason`), admitted with
`dispatch` (or operator) on a request not yet answered. Both are
defined from `seed/6`, the register entry naming them: a `seed/5`
validator's unknown-verb arm fails a chain carrying either. Refused: a
request that becomes a contract without a dispatcher's act; a body in
the payload; a third verb.

**D2. A request grants nothing and obliges the dispatcher.**
`request.filed` needs standing only, like `message.sent`; the ingress
identity (a mirror's or dashboard's service key, or a federation
remote's ingress key) holds no capability, and attribution is not
trust. An unanswered request is an obligation kind owed to the
dispatch lane (`obligations.md`), surfacing in the situation read as a
notice — origin, kind, size, position, age — never the summary; the
projections carry the payload verbatim, as they carry everything, and
the report counts requests by kind and outcome and the answer latency.
Refused: the summary in the read; a request answered by anyone but a
dispatch-granted key.

**D3. The mirror arm closes with the corpus fired at the ingress.**
The injection suite gains the request arm: every corpus file planted
in `summary`, `reference` (a valid ref form with hostile text is not
a ref, so the shape refuses it, which is the assertion), `origin` and
the subject; the dispatcher's reachable set with pending hostile
requests is exactly its pinned set; the situation read carries the
marker nowhere; `request.answered` over a hostile request yields only
what the dispatcher's allowlist already permitted. III.J row 2 is then
met in full, and the lanes spec says so in place of the residual.

**D4. Uniform read remotes are a declared list and one command.**
`seed.json` gains `federation: {"remotes": [{"name", "remote", "ref"}]}`,
strict, read-only. `seed federation report --config <file> --state
<dir>` fetches every remote's ledger ref with the gitref client into
the state dir, verifies each chain from its own genesis under its own
keyring (no key crosses), folds each, and renders `federation.json`:
per remote, the name, tip, count, protocol, halt state, contracts by
state, open escalations and unanswered requests, and the org totals.
Byte-identical on the same tips; a remote that does not verify is
reported as such, never folded. Refused: a super-ledger; any verb
whose subject or payload names another ledger; a federation command
that takes a key.

**D5. Cross-repo work enters the target ledger as a request.** A
source deployment's contract is proposed to a target as
`request.filed` with `kind: cross-repo`, `origin` the source's name in
the target's federation block, `reference` `<source-name>/<contract>
@ <source tip>`; the ingress key is enrolled in the target as a
service with no capability. The target's dispatcher answers as for
any request. Nothing flows back: the source reads the target's
answer through its own read remote. Refused: a write path in either
direction (proven by absence: the verb catalog, the keyring
vocabulary and the capability audit contain nothing that names
another ledger, and the drill asserts a federation read appends
nothing).

**D6. Drills.** The boundary: both verbs' shapes, `seed/6` by
version, standing for the filing, `dispatch` for the answer, a
subject that resolves to nothing, an answer to an answered request,
`filed` without `intent`. The obligation and the read: an unanswered
request owed to the dispatcher with its age, the notice without the
summary. The corpus: D3's arm, in the admit suite and at the terminal
(`cmd/seed/injection_cli_test.go`). Federation: two ledgers in one
test with distinct roots, the org view over both, a tampered remote
reported and not folded, byte-identical output on unchanged tips, and
the absence proof (no append in the state dir; `seed federation` has
no `--key`). Cross-repo: a source contract proposed into a target,
answered by the target's dispatcher with an intent citing it. The
projections byte-identical on chains without requests; the trajectory
corpus replays green (the dispatcher lane's frame gains no act until
its manifest declares `request.answered`, which this card adds with
the corpus re-recorded on purpose). Mutation evidence: a request
admitted with a body; an answer by a claim-only key; a summary in the
situation read; a federation read that appends; a corpus file that
widens the dispatcher's set.

**D7. Bounds.** The verbs are the ingress; the mirrors and dashboards
that file them are adapters outside this card (v1's mirror plan and
the flavors are where they live). The A2A-shaped boundary (Phase 13
item 5) is built on D5's request and D4's read. No lane obeys a
request: the dispatcher's act is its own, under its own allowlist.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **The ingress verbs exist and change nothing.** `request.filed` and
   `request.answered` admit at `seed/6` with their strict shapes, a
   `seed/5` validator refuses a chain carrying them by version, and no
   state moves on either.
2. **A request grants nothing and is owed an answer.** The filing
   needs standing only; the answer needs `dispatch`; an unanswered
   request is an obligation on the dispatch lane with its age; the
   situation read shows the notice and never the summary.
3. **The mirror arm is met.** The corpus fired at `request.filed`
   widens the dispatcher's reachable set by nothing, the marker
   appears in no read, and the lanes spec records III.J row 2 as met.
4. **Read remotes are uniform and read-only.** `seed federation
   report` renders the org view over two ledgers verified under their
   own keyrings, byte-identical on unchanged tips, a tampered remote
   reported and not folded, and appends nothing anywhere.
5. **Cross-repo work enters as a request.** A source contract
   proposed into a target is answered by the target's dispatcher with
   an intent citing it, and nothing flows back but a read.
6. **Mutation evidence.** Each fails a drill: a request with a body; an
   answer by a claim-only key; the summary in the read; a federation
   read that appends; a corpus file that widens the set.
7. `make check` green with coverage measured cold, three readings
   above the gate; no model identifiers in any committed artifact.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- Every pre-existing chain verifies byte for byte; `seed/6` is
  additive; every projection is byte-identical on chains carrying no
  request; `message.sent` and the notices are unchanged; the
  dispatcher's allowlist is unchanged; the trajectory corpus replays
  green after its deliberate re-recording.
