# os-5c8a312c — Phase 12 item 2: the forge-hosted admission service and the protections reconciler

Build plan Phase 12 item 2. The charter names three admission deployments
and requires every deployment to declare one (§II.2). Two exist in the
tree: the hook form (`seed-admit`, Phase 2.3) and cooperative. The third,
**enforced, forge-hosted** — "the ledger ref is written only by a
dedicated admission identity (a small service or serverless endpoint that
actors propose to), and forge protections make that identity the ref's
sole writer; the service holds no durable state" — is declared today and
answered by one honest sentence, `posture.ForgeHostedGap`. This card
lands the service, the forge protections as declared desired-state
reconciled by command (§II.14), and the drills that make the third
posture as real as the first. Conformance: III.B rows 1 and 3, III.L row
5, III.P row 2.

## What the tree actually shows

- **One derivation, one caller so far.** `seed-admit` (`next/cmd/seed-admit`)
  reads the pre-receive protocol and judges a pushed range with
  `admitUpdate(gitDir, old, new)`: one `VerifyFromGenesis` over the
  pushed stream, a record-level prefix check, then `admit.Run` over the
  new records with the boundary's share of `admit.Default()` selected by
  exclusion. #241 (Phase 12 item 1, in review) adds a code-ref half to
  the same binary: non-ledger refs decided from the ledger at the
  guarded ref's tip and a pusher identity the transport asserts
  (`SEED_PUSHER`), and `seed.json` gains a `protected` path list read
  from the default branch's tip. Nothing in either half is
  hook-specific except the stdin protocol.
- **The client already separates judging from pushing.**
  `gitref.AppendLoop` fetches, materializes, verifies, re-links, re-signs,
  validates through `admit.Check`, and only then `CommitAndPush`es;
  `openRemoteSession` (`cmd/seed/remote.go`) is the one prologue every
  remote verb shares (`ledger append --remote`, the loop verbs, `verdict
  render --remote`). Read access is git; only the last step writes.
- **The posture is one strict field.** `posture.Config` is
  `{"posture": <name>}` in `seed.json`, read by `seed doctor --config`;
  `ForgeHostedGap` is printed for the third posture and nothing else
  differs by posture on the client.
- **Forges protect branches and tags, not custom namespaces.** GitHub
  rulesets target `refs/heads/*` and `refs/tags/*`; `refs/seed/ledger`
  is neither. v1 learned this: its ledger is the `seed-state` branch
  under a ruleset. The hook posture never needed the forge's help, so
  the custom namespace cost nothing there.
- **v1's protections are a checklist, not a reconciler.** `scripts/seed
  init-github` prints what to click and says the engine cannot call the
  API.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

**D1. The service is `seed-admit serve`: the same binary, the same
judgment.** A second entry mode beside the hook, not a second program:
`seed-admit serve --remote <url> --ref <ledger ref> --state <dir>
--listen <addr>`. For each proposal it fetches the ledger ref into its
clone, materializes the tip, appends the proposed record(s) into a
candidate tree, commits, and judges old→candidate with the very
`admitUpdate` the hook runs — the ledger ref only: no proposal names a
code ref, and under this posture code refs are the forge's protections'
business (D5); an admitted candidate is pushed fast-forward under the
identity the service's git credential carries. The
service signs nothing (records are actor-signed; it relays), retries
nothing (a lost push race is reported as the race it is and the
proposer's loop re-links and proposes again), and keeps no state but its
clone, which a replacement instance rebuilds from the remote. Refused: a
service that re-links or re-signs on the proposer's behalf — the
signature over `prev` is what makes a race detectable, and only the key
holder can produce it.

**D2. The proposal protocol is one proposal endpoint speaking the
envelope, beside a health probe.**
`POST /propose` with a JSON body `{ref, records: [<wire record>...]}` in
`protocol.md`'s record encoding; the response body is the
`seed-envelope/0` the CLI would have printed — the boundary's own code,
reason and position stamp — with the HTTP status mapping declared in the
spec (200 admitted; 409 the ref moved, retry; 422 refused with the
envelope; 503 the service cannot reach the remote). `GET /healthz`, the
probe, reports the service's remote, ref, and last verified position for
the doctor and carries no proposal. No authentication of proposers at the transport: the record's
signature is the authentication, exactly as at the hook. Refused: a
second wire format for records, or a transport-level identity the
boundary would then have to trust.

**D3. The client changes its last step and nothing else.** `gitref`
gains a `Proposer` (an interface with one method, `Propose(ref string,
recs []*event.Record) (*Result, error)`) and `Client.WithProposer`; when
set, `attempt` replaces `CommitAndPush` with the proposal and maps a 409
onto `ErrNonFastForward` so `AppendLoop`'s retry is unchanged, and a 422
onto a typed remote refusal carrying the service's envelope so the CLI
renders the boundary's own code rather than a generic `remote_rejected`.
`openRemoteSession` reads the deployment declaration (`--config`,
defaulting to `seed.json` as the doctor does) and, when the posture is
`enforced-forge-hosted`, requires `admission.endpoint` and wires the
proposer; under the other postures the declaration changes nothing. The
client still self-validates before proposing (the cooperative half is
what every posture keeps). Refused: a per-verb `--propose` flag — the
posture is the deployment's declaration, not an argument a caller
remembers.

**D4. Under the forge-hosted posture the ledger ref lives in the branch
namespace.** `refs/heads/seed-ledger` by default, named by the
declaration's `admission.ledger_ref`, because that is the namespace a
forge can restrict updates on; the hook posture keeps `refs/seed/ledger`
untouched. The ref name is already a parameter of every ledger seam
(`SEED_ADMIT_REF`, `--ref`), so this is a declaration, not a code path.
The spec says why in one sentence so nobody "fixes" it back.

**D5. The desired state is the declaration, extended, never a second
file.** `seed.json` (`posture.Config`, checked in at the repo root and
read at the default branch's tip, the shape #241 establishes) gains an
`admission` block, required under `enforced-forge-hosted` and refused
under the others: `{endpoint, identity, ledger_ref, checks: [<required
status check names>], reviews: <n>, owners: [<forge identities>]}` —
`identity` being the forge login or app slug the service's credential
belongs to, `owners` the identities that review the protected surface. The reconciler derives
the forge's desired state from that block plus the charter's rules, with
no second source: the ledger branch updatable by `identity` alone, no
force-push, no deletion; the default branch requiring `checks`,
`reviews`, conversation resolution, no force-push, no deletion — the
default branch being the repository's `HEAD` symref, read and never
declared, as #241's hook reads it; release tags immutable (create-only); contract branches `seed/*` protected from
force-push and deletion; and `protected` (#241's list) rendered as the
`CODEOWNERS` entry naming the governance root's forge identity
(`admission.owners`), written to the working tree for a reviewed PR,
never pushed. Strict decoding as `posture.Load` does today; an unknown
field refuses; a forge-hosted declaration missing the block refuses
`posture_invalid` naming the block.

**D6. The reconciler is `seed protections plan | apply`, over a forge
adapter with a credential-free arm.** `internal/protections`: the
desired-state derivation (D5), a `Current` model, a `Diff`, and a `Forge`
interface (`Read() (Current, error)`, `Apply(Diff) error`). Two
adapters: `github` over the REST rulesets API with `net/http` and a token
from the environment (`GITHUB_TOKEN`), and `snapshot`, which reads
`Current` from a JSON file and writes the applied result back — the arm
CI and the drills run with no network and no secret. `plan` prints the diff as an envelope (drift by name; exit 0 with
`drift: 0`, otherwise exit **28** `drift` — a new code, allocated here:
a declared desired state and an observed state differ, the message
naming each difference — refined `protections_drift`, so a CI job can
gate on it); `apply` performs it and re-reads. A rule the forge
cannot express is reported by name as `manual` with the click it needs,
never dropped silently — the honest residual the charter's "risk-limit
honesty where enforcement is impossible" asks for. Refused: shelling
out to `gh`; a reconciler that applies without first planning.

**D7. The doctor reports the deployment it can see, and the gap sentence
retires.** Under `enforced-forge-hosted` the doctor prints the endpoint,
the ledger ref and the identity from the declaration, probes `/healthz`
when `--probe` is given, and, when `--current <snapshot>` is given, the
protections drift count; `ForgeHostedGap` is deleted with the posture it
apologized for. The cooperative consequence is unchanged.

**D8. Drills, and what each proves.** (a) The shared adversary table
(#99's `TestDrillRawAdversaryPerPosture`) gains the third posture:
the fixture is a bare remote with NO hook whose ruleset stand-in is a
fixture-side pre-receive refusing any ledger-branch update whose
`SEED_PUSHER` is not the admission identity — labeled in the test as the
model of the forge's rule, which is what it is — and a `seed-admit serve`
subprocess; the actor's raw push is refused with the ref unmoved
(III.B's attempted-direct-push row under this posture), the same hostile
content proposed through the service is refused with the SAME envelope
code the hook posture gives, and a valid proposal is admitted with the
ref advanced by the service alone. (b) Kill-and-replace: a second
instance from a fresh clone judges the whole table identically. (c) One
derivation: every row of the table run through the hook, the service and
`admit.Check` in-process agrees on code and reason. (d) The race: two
proposers over one tip; the loser receives 409, its `AppendLoop`
re-links and lands second, and a proposal with a stale `prev` never
refuses as `chain_invalid`. (e) The reconciler: plan against snapshot
fixtures with drift, without drift, and with a rule the snapshot forge
declares unexpressible; apply through an `httptest` forge modeling the
rulesets endpoints; the CODEOWNERS rendering; the CI-identity lint
(a workflow under `.github/workflows` declaring `contents: write` with
a schedule trigger is drift). (f) The doctor per posture, `--probe`
against the fixture service, the missing-block and unknown-field
refusals. (g) Mutation evidence: the service admitting on refusal;
judging the candidate against the new tip instead of the old; retrying
a race on the proposer's behalf; re-signing; the client pushing when a
proposer is set; the reconciler applying without a plan; a dropped
unexpressible rule; the gap sentence surviving.

**D9. Bounds, stated.** Hosting, TLS, and the credential the service
pushes with are the deployment's (the spec's deployment note says what
to give the service and what never to give it: no actor key). The
mapping of Seed actors to forge identities beyond `identity` and
`owners` is not attempted: contract-branch exclusivity stays Seed's
fence at admission (and #241's code-ref half under the hook posture),
and the forge posture's residual — a forge cannot know which key holds
a claim — is recorded in the spec rather than approximated. Only the
GitHub adapter ships; the interface is what item 13.3 (the non-primary
forge adapter) implements next.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **The service enforces with the hook's judgment.** A valid proposal is
   admitted and the ledger branch advances under the service's identity;
   a refusable proposal is refused with the boundary's own envelope code
   and the ref unmoved; a proposal whose `prev` is not the current tip
   answers 409 and never `chain_invalid`; the service holds no state a
   fresh clone does not rebuild (D8(b)).
2. **The third posture is drilled on the shared table.** D8(a) and (c):
   every row of #99's adversary table yields the same code and reason
   through the hook, the service and `admit.Check`; the actor's direct
   push is refused by the fixture's ruleset stand-in with the ref
   unmoved; the cooperative row is unchanged.
3. **The client is posture-driven.** With `seed.json` declaring
   `enforced-forge-hosted`, every remote verb that shares
   `openRemoteSession` proposes rather than pushes, renders the service's
   code on refusal, and lands second after a 409 (D8(d)); with the other
   postures the declaration changes nothing and every existing remote
   drill passes unmodified.
4. **The declaration is strict.** The `admission` block is required
   under `enforced-forge-hosted` and refused under the others, unknown
   fields refuse, and a forge-hosted declaration without it refuses
   `posture_invalid` naming the block.
5. **The reconciler plans before it applies.** `plan` reports drift by
   name (exit 28 `drift`, refined `protections_drift`) or none (exit 0), `apply` performs
   the plan through the adapter and re-reads to a clean plan, an
   unexpressible rule is reported as `manual` with the click it needs,
   the CODEOWNERS rendering names the protected list and the owners,
   and the CI-identity lint flags a scheduled workflow with `contents:
   write`. The `snapshot` adapter runs all of it credential-free in CI;
   the `github` adapter is drilled against an `httptest` forge.
6. **The doctor reports the deployment.** Endpoint, identity and ledger
   ref under the third posture, `--probe` against the fixture service,
   `--current` drift, the gap sentence gone; the cooperative consequence
   still printed verbatim.
7. **Mutation evidence.** Each mutation in D8(g) turns a named drill red
   and is restored by name.
8. `make check` green with coverage measured cold, three readings above
   the gate; the suites pass unprivileged; no model identifiers in any
   committed artifact.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- The hook posture is byte-for-byte unchanged in behavior: every
  `seed-admit` drill (#94, #99, #241's if merged first) passes as before,
  `refs/seed/ledger` stays its default, and `admitUpdate`'s judgment is
  reused, not edited.
- No new verb, transition row, capability or protocol version; every
  pre-existing chain verifies byte for byte; every projection build is
  byte-identical on chains this card does not create.
- Every remote drill under the self-hosted and cooperative postures
  passes with no flag added; `seed doctor` under those postures prints
  what it printed.
