# Plan: next — Phase 11 item 2, the promotion gate (support from more than one actor where the family allows, applies-when, provenance, last-validated); adversarial evaluation for behavior-changing lessons; the contested state; lessons delivered at claim time (os-96850e5a)

The build plan's Phase 11 item 2: *"Promotion gate: ≥2-trajectory
support, applies-when, provenance, last-validated; adversarial
evaluation for behavior-changing lessons; contested state. The
applies-when carries its delivery moment: a promoted lesson whose
applies-when matches the claimed subject surfaces in the claim packet
and in the response envelope at claim time (the charter III.I row the
Phase 8 exit routes here — knowledge nobody is shown at the right
moment is knowledge that does not exist)."* The charter (§12):
hypotheses carry *"applies-when conditions, supporting contract ids,
exceptions, provenance. Minimum support: more than one non-failed
trajectory, from more than one actor where the family allows it"*;
validation *"runs the hypothesis against held-out evidence and, for
behavior-changing lessons, an adversarial evaluation: does the lesson
survive deliberately constructed counter-trajectories? Conflicting
evidence is a first-class contested state (never silently averaged;
contested lessons do not surface in envelopes)"*; promotion *"is a PR
through the normal gates … The curator proposes; gates dispose"*.
Conformance III.K rows 3 and 5 and III.I row 5 are the targets.

This plan is written against item 1's stores (os-f30ee0d3, #226) and
the evals of Phase 10 item 2 (os-03e47abb, #221), and depends on both
merging first. Items 3 (the poisoning drill), 4 (expiry, retirement,
rollback) and 5 (the flywheel) build on it.

## What the tree actually shows

Measured against `main` with #226's design laid over it:

- **Item 1 gives the stages their facts and a structural minimum.**
  `hypothesis.proposed` on `h-<12 hex>` from a `curate` key carries
  `{claim, applies_when, support, exceptions, provenance}` and admits
  only when `support` cites two or more admitted observations on two
  or more distinct non-failed contracts, recording the citing actors;
  `lesson.promoted` cites an admitted hypothesis by position; lesson
  files under `next/knowledge/lessons/` carry a frontmatter whose
  presence item 1 lints and whose content it leaves to this card; the
  `knowledge` projection knows `proposed` and `promoted` and reserves
  `contested`; `curate` is disjoint from `claim` and `operator`.
- **`applies_when` is a string nobody parses**, so nothing can match
  a lesson to a contract; the fold reads `tier` and `budget` from the
  intent and not `routing`.
- **Nothing contests.** The fold has no stage between proposed and
  promoted, and no verb carries counter-evidence; the only way to
  disagree with a hypothesis is to not promote it, which is silence.
- **Evals already turn a constructed scenario into an authenticated
  known verdict through the production verbs** (`evals.md`): a
  definition under `next/evals/<name>/`, filed with the `eval` marker,
  worked and judged like any contract, its pass authenticated at the
  boundary (`AuthenticPass`, replayed at its own position). III.K's
  "deliberately constructed counter-trajectories" have exactly that
  shape, and the eval machinery does not care what a fixture is for:
  the marker names an eval and a tuple and nothing about what the
  eval was filed to prove.
- **Claim time has two reads and one provisioned handoff.** `claim
  take` answers with `{subject}` and the affordances; the orienting
  read (`seed situation`) is the position-stamped read a lane wakes
  to; the ledger packet is the deliberate EXIT's (`packets.md`),
  written by the leaving worker; and the executor's `Provision`
  writes the handoff packet bytes it is given into the workspace at
  `.seed-run/packet.json` (`executors.md`), which is the packet a
  fresh executor actually receives. The ledger packet's four-part
  shape refuses unknown keys at the top level.
- **Observations are attested, and readers verify.** `plan.approved`
  and `merge.observed` are forge facts an observer or operator
  attests and the boundary takes on shape; what the record cannot
  prove (a merge happened, a file exists at an anchor) is proven by
  the reader that holds the repository: the receipt verifier, `seed
  reconcile`'s evidence grade. A lesson fact is the same kind of fact
  with a sharper consequence, since what it names is handed to a
  worker.
- **Shipped content is linted by drills, not by the Makefile**: the
  spec-mirror tests are what `make check` runs, and the Makefile is a
  protected path. `next/knowledge/lessons/` ships empty from item 1
  and is read by nobody.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — applies-when is a predicate over record-derivable fields.**
  `applies_when` becomes an object with at least one of `routing`
  (equal to the subject's intent routing; the fold gains `Routing`),
  `tier` (equal to the subject's tier) and `paths` (a non-empty list;
  one entry must prefix the subject's acceptance ref path); present
  fields conjoin; an empty object, an unknown field or a malformed
  value refuses at `hypothesis.proposed` naming the part. That
  tightens item 1's free text to the one shape a claim-time match can
  evaluate from the fold alone; no chain carries a hypothesis before
  this card, so the change is additive in effect. The predicate lives
  in `internal/curation` (`AppliesWhen`, `Selects(state)`), one
  implementation for the boundary, the lint and the delivery.

- **D2 — the support rule gains the actor arm.** At
  `hypothesis.proposed`, the holders of the cited observations (the
  signer of the `claim.taken` whose window each cited dead end or
  packet finding fell in) must be two or more distinct keys **where
  the family allows it**: the family is the set of contracts the
  applies-when selects in the record, and it allows when those
  contracts have two or more distinct holders over the chain. A
  family with one holder admits support from that one holder, and the
  fold records `single_actor_family` on the hypothesis so the
  projection says why the arm was not applied; a family with two
  holders refuses a two-contract support from one of them naming the
  arm. Item 1's distinct-contract and non-failed arms stand unchanged.

- **D3 — the contested state is an attributable fact, never an
  average.** New verb **`hypothesis.contested`** (`curation.*`,
  accepted by `curate` alone, the no-fallback posture of the proposal)
  on the hypothesis subject: `{"hypothesis": "h@position",
  "evidence": ["<contract>@<position>", …], "reason"}`. The boundary
  requires each evidence to be an admitted observation (a dead end or
  a packet finding) on a contract the applies-when selects that is
  NOT in the support set: held-out evidence, by construction. The
  fold moves the hypothesis to `contested` from `proposed` or
  `promoted`; a contested hypothesis refuses `lesson.promoted`, and a
  promoted lesson whose hypothesis is contested stops surfacing (D6)
  while its file and every fact remain, which is "evidence kept". A
  contested hypothesis is never edited or averaged back: the way out
  is a new `hypothesis.proposed` whose claim or exceptions differ,
  citing the counter-evidence as an exception and judged on its own
  support. For that road to exist the hypothesis id must derive from
  the canonical claim AND exceptions, not the claim alone: item 1's
  derivation is widened by this card, so a re-proposal that changes
  nothing is still the duplicate the boundary refuses, and one that
  adds an exception is a new subject. `seed knowledge
  validate --hypothesis <h@position>` is the held-out listing: the
  observations on selected contracts outside the support set, for
  the curator to judge, then contest or promote. The residual is
  stated in `curation.md`: no machine decides that an outcome
  contradicts a claim; a contest is a curator's attributable
  judgment over evidence the record already holds.

- **D4 — the promotion gate has a ledger half and a file half.**
  `lesson.promoted` grows to `{lesson, hypothesis, pr, carrier,
  adversarial?, last_validated, expires}`, `carrier` one of
  `knowledge`, `role`, `skill`, `workflow`, `harness`, `adversarial`
  `{"eval": "<name>", "verdict": "<position>"}`, and the two stamps
  RFC3339 with `expires` after `last_validated`: the fact carries what
  the frontmatter says so that item 4's expiry is derivable from the
  fold without the file. The **ledger half** (record-derivable, at
  admission): the cited hypothesis is admitted and not contested at
  the tip, its support satisfies D2, `carrier` is a member, the stamps
  are present and ordered, and `adversarial` is REQUIRED for every
  carrier: its verdict is an authenticated pass, replayed at its own
  position, on a subject whose `eval` marker names that eval AND
  binds it to this hypothesis and to this lesson anchor (D5), filed
  after the hypothesis's position. Every promoted lesson is
  behavior-changing, because promotion is what puts it in front of a
  worker at claim time; a `knowledge` carrier says where the lesson
  lands, never that it is harmless, so no carrier is exempt (review
  finding on #228). The fact also carries **`digest`**, the sha256 of
  the lesson file's bytes at the anchor, which is what a reader
  verifies before surfacing (D6). The **file half**, `seed knowledge lint
  <file> --ledger <dir> --repo <dir> [--now]`: the frontmatter agrees
  with the fact (hypothesis anchor, carrier, adversarial, both
  stamps), the file's bytes at the anchor hash to the fact's `digest`
  and the anchor commit is an ancestor of the repository's head (the
  promotion PR merged), its
  `applies-when` parses as D1 and equals the hypothesis's, `support`
  equals the hypothesis's, every `provenance` anchor resolves in the
  repository at its commit, `last-validated` is RFC3339 and not after
  the declared instant, `expires` is after it. A drill runs the lint
  over `next/knowledge/lessons/` with the shipped ledger fixtures, so
  `make check` refuses a shipped lesson that fails it: that is the PR
  gate the charter routes promotion through, on the surface the tree
  already gates that way. Every refusal of the proposal rule, the
  contest rule, the promotion rule's ledger half and the lint's file
  half is a `curation.GateError` naming a gate registered in
  `curation.Gates()` at init, and the constructor refuses an
  unregistered name: the registry is the one authority item 3's
  poisoning drill enumerates to derive its coverage, so a gate added
  here without a poison there is a red test rather than a silent gap.

- **D5 — the adversarial evaluation is an eval, bound to the
  candidate.** A counter-trajectory is an eval definition under
  `next/evals/<name>/` whose fixture is the situation the lesson
  claims to improve, constructed so that the known verdict fails if
  the lesson is wrong. It is filed with `seed eval file --for-lesson
  <h@position> --carrier <path @ commit>`, and the filing's `eval`
  marker grows to `{"name", "tuple"?, "lesson", "carrier"}`: the
  hypothesis and the exact candidate revision the eval is for, on the
  record at filing. It is worked in a workspace that includes the
  carrier revision and judged by the verifier, and `seed verdict
  render` on a subject whose marker names a carrier refuses
  (refining **`carrier_absent`** under `checks_red`) unless the
  carrier commit is an ancestor of the submission head: the lesson
  was actually applied. The promotion boundary then requires the
  cited verdict's subject to carry a marker whose `lesson` equals the
  promoted hypothesis and whose `carrier` equals the promoted
  `lesson` anchor, so a pass on an eval filed for another hypothesis,
  another revision, or nothing in particular is not survival (review
  finding on #228). Nothing else runs: the machinery is Phase 10 item
  2's, the anchor rule binds the definition to its reviewed revision,
  and the qualification rule's authenticity check is reused as is. No
  definition is shipped for this; the drills build theirs in
  temporary repositories the way the eval drills do.

- **D6 — delivery is derived from the fold and verified against the
  repository before anything surfaces.** The surfacing set is every
  promoted lesson whose hypothesis is not contested (item 4 adds not
  retired and not expired), whose applies-when selects the subject,
  AND whose fact resolves in the repository the reader holds: the
  anchor commit is an ancestor of the repository's head (the
  promotion PR merged) and the file's bytes at the anchor hash to the
  fact's `digest`. A fact that does not resolve is reported as
  `lessons_unresolved` (anchor and reason) and never surfaces, so an
  attested promotion naming a missing, unmerged or altered file
  reaches no worker (review finding on #228); `seed reconcile`
  classifies the same facts **`lesson_unverified`** at evidence grade.
  Three surfaces carry the set from one derivation
  (`curation.Surfacing(ctx, repo, subject)`): the **envelope** of
  `claim take` gains `lessons` (`[{lesson, hypothesis, applies_when,
  digest}]`, present on every claim, empty when nothing matches; the
  loop already holds the repository through `WithRepo`); the
  **provisioned packet**: `executor.ProvisionSpec` gains `Lessons`
  and `Provision` writes them to `.seed-run/lessons.json` beside
  `packet.json`, so the handoff a fresh executor receives carries
  them (a sibling file rather than a fifth key, because the ledger
  packet's four-part shape refuses unknown keys and the provisioned
  directory is the handoff, not the fact); and the **orienting read**:
  `seed situation --repo <dir>` lists the same rows per held subject
  and without `--repo` reports only the count as `lessons_unverified`,
  naming the flag. Contested lessons appear on none of them.

- **D7 — projection and versioning.** The `knowledge` projection
  gains the `contested` stage, `single_actor_family`, and per lesson
  `surfaces: true|false` with the reason; the report's knowledge
  section counts contested. One new verb and payload fields are
  catalog growth under an existing namespace (item 1's D5 posture):
  no version bump, every existing chain verifies byte for byte.

- **D8 — scope guard.** No expiry, retirement or rollback (item 4); no
  poisoning corpus (item 3 assembles it from this card's refusals);
  no flywheel (item 5); no role, skill or workflow patch mechanics
  (the carrier's own PR goes through the existing gates; this card
  gates the lesson fact and its file); no model call; no automatic
  contest; no Makefile change; no change to `transitions.json`,
  `internal/loop` or `next/lanes`; the eval machinery changes only in
  the filing's bound marker and the render's carrier check.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **The predicate.** `applies_when` with `routing`, `tier` or `paths`
   parses; an empty object, an unknown field, an empty `paths` or a
   non-string value refuses at `hypothesis.proposed` naming the part;
   `Selects` matches a subject on every present field and on no
   absent one, `paths` by prefix of the acceptance ref path.
2. **The actor arm.** In a family with two holders, a support of two
   contracts held by one key refuses naming the arm and one held by
   two admits; in a family with one holder the same support admits
   and the fold records `single_actor_family`; the family is computed
   from the selection, never from the support set.
3. **The contest.** `hypothesis.contested` admits from a `curate` key
   citing held-out observations on selected contracts; it refuses
   citing a support-set observation, an unselected contract, a
   position that is no observation, from a `claim` key, and from a
   governance root holding no `curate`; the fold reads `contested`;
   `lesson.promoted` on it refuses; contesting a promoted hypothesis
   leaves the file and the facts and removes the lesson from the
   surfacing set.
4. **The gate's ledger half.** `lesson.promoted` refuses without
   `carrier`, with an unknown carrier, without either stamp, with
   `expires` not after `last_validated`, without `digest`, without
   `adversarial` for ANY carrier (`knowledge` included), citing a
   fail, citing a pass on a subject without the eval marker or with
   another eval's, citing a pass whose marker binds another
   hypothesis or another carrier revision, citing a pass filed before
   the hypothesis, and citing a raw-pushed pass a later-granted key
   signed; it admits any carrier with an authenticated pass bound to
   this hypothesis and this lesson anchor. `seed eval file
   --for-lesson --carrier` puts the binding on the marker, and `seed
   verdict render` on such a subject refuses `carrier_absent` when
   the carrier commit is not an ancestor of the submission head.
   Every refusal above, and every lint refusal below, is a `GateError`
   naming a registered gate, and `curation.Gates()` lists exactly the
   gates the rules construct.
5. **The gate's file half.** `seed knowledge lint` refuses a lesson
   whose frontmatter cites another hypothesis, whose applies-when
   fails to parse or differs from the hypothesis's, whose support
   differs, whose provenance anchor the repository lacks, whose file
   at the anchor does not hash to the fact's `digest`, whose anchor
   commit is not an ancestor of the head, whose last-validated is
   after `--now`, whose expires precedes it, or whose carrier
   disagrees with the fact, each naming the part; the
   shipped store passes, and the drill over it is what `make check`
   runs.
6. **Delivery.** After a promotion whose applies-when selects the
   subject and whose file resolves, `claim take` returns `lessons`
   carrying the anchor and digest; a non-matching subject returns
   `[]`; a contested hypothesis's lesson is absent; a fact whose
   anchor is not an ancestor of the head, or whose file bytes do not
   hash to its digest, is reported in `lessons_unresolved` and absent
   from `lessons`, and `seed reconcile` classifies it
   `lesson_unverified`; `Provision` writes the same set to
   `.seed-run/lessons.json` beside `packet.json`, an empty list when
   nothing matches; `seed situation --repo` lists the same rows for
   the held subject and nothing for another, and without `--repo`
   reports `lessons_unverified` with no anchors; `seed knowledge
   validate` lists the held-out observations and nothing from the
   support set.
7. **End to end.** In small-team mode two workers record dead ends on
   two contracts, the curator proposes, an eval filed for that
   hypothesis and candidate revision passes, the observer promotes
   citing it, the next claim on a matching contract receives the
   lesson in its envelope and in its provisioned `lessons.json`, and
   a contest removes it from the next claim after that.
8. **Mutation evidence.** Each must fail a drill: the predicate
   matching on an absent field; the arm counting one holder as two;
   the family computed from the support set; a contest accepted from
   the support set or from a `claim` key; a contested hypothesis
   promotable; `adversarial` optional for any carrier; the marker's
   `lesson` or `carrier` not compared at promotion; the render's
   ancestry check skipped; the pass not replayed at its position; a
   fail accepted as survival; delivery surfacing a contested lesson;
   delivery surfacing a fact whose digest or ancestry does not verify;
   the provisioner omitting `lessons.json`; the lint skipping
   provenance resolution or the digest; a refusal raised outside the
   gate registry; the projection omitting `contested`.
9. `make check` green with coverage measured **cold**, at least three
   readings above the gate, and the suites pass **unprivileged** under
   `setpriv --reuid=65534 --regid=65534 --clear-groups`.
