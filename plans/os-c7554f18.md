# os-c7554f18 — Phase 13 item 7: tuple ranking as supervisor policy

Build plan Phase 13 item 7: tuple ranking as supervisor policy — eval
results (mints, spot-checks, calibration agreement) rank qualified
tuples, and the planner lane's offers carry the strongest into the
`tuples` scope Phase 10 item 1 landed as the scheduling input.
Charter III.J row 3's policy clause ("the planner lane receives the
strongest tuples by policy"), §II.9 ("Scheduling inputs: … grants and
qualification tuples … executor cost class (mechanical work to cheap
tuples, hard work to strong ones)"), routed here by the Phase 10 exit
record. Deps: Phase 12; Phase 10 items 1–4.

## What the tree actually shows

- **Qualification is a set, unranked.** `actor.qualified` and
  `actor.disqualified` are the keyring's facts (`keyring.Qualification`:
  capability, tuple, the eval contract and the verdict cited); the set
  rule reads them at `run.started`; `seed eval status|act`
  (`internal/eval.Due`) derives what the chain owes — offers for ready
  evals, mints for authenticated passes whose receipt recomputes,
  disqualifications for fails, spot-checks by age, calibration for
  verifier keys against a gold held outside the tree.
- **Offers carry tuples as a scheduling input, written by hand.**
  `offer.published`'s eligibility has the `tuples` scope
  (`next/spec/offers.md`): the supervisor names the configurations it
  wants, and `qualification.md` says in so many words that this is
  "the whole of 'strongest tuples by policy' the tree can honestly hold
  until item 2's eval results exist to rank them". They exist now.
- **Nothing ranks.** Every qualified tuple is equal in an offer; the
  evals `Due` publishes are unscoped; no projection or doctor line
  says which configuration the evidence favors.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

**D1. The ranking is a record-derived policy table.** `internal/ranking`
(new) derives, from the keyring's qualifications and the fold's eval
facts alone, an ordered list per capability of the qualified
`(tuple)` configurations and the evidence behind each: mints (the
authenticated passes cited by `actor.qualified`, with the latest's
`ts`), spot-check passes (re-tests minted after the first), and for
`verdict` tuples calibration passes; the score is the count of
qualifying evidence, the tie rule the latest pass's `ts` (newer
first) and then the tuple's canonical string. A tuple whose latest
fact is a disqualification, or whose every holder is suspended or
revoked, is **not ranked**: absent, not last. Calibration
**agreement** (the scorecard-versus-gold figure) refines the verdict
ranking only when the gold is supplied to the derivation
(`--gold`, as `seed eval status` takes it); without it the ranking is
by passes alone and says so. Refused: a clock read (age is measured
against the declared instant, `--as-of`); a weight the table does not
state; a suspended or disqualified tuple anywhere in the list.

**D2. The planner lane's offers carry the strongest.** `seed offer
publish` gains `--strongest <n>` (with `--capability`), which fills the
`tuples` scope with the top `n` of the ranking for that capability at
the offer's instant and refuses when the ranking is empty (an
unscoped offer is the supervisor's explicit choice, never a fallback);
`eval.Due`'s offers for ready evals and for the contracts routed to
the planner lane carry the top of the `claim` ranking the same way,
so `seed eval act` publishes them by policy. The offer payload is
unchanged: the scope is the input Phase 10 item 1 defined, now filled
by policy. Refused: a new offer field; an offer naming a tuple outside
the ranking.

**D3. The ranking is visible.** The `ranking` projection (new,
version 1): `ranking.json` per capability, the ordered tuples with
their evidence and the derivation's instant, byte-identical on the
same chain; `seed doctor` names the top tuple per capability; the
report's `lanes` section gains the planner's `strongest` (the tuple
its latest offer carried). Refused: a projection that reads the gold
(the gold is outside the tree; the projection ranks by passes and
says `agreement: null`).

**D4. Drills.** The ranking changes only when eval facts change
(byte-identical across unrelated appends); a disqualified tuple
leaves the ranking at once; a suspended holder's tuple leaves and
returns with re-enrollment; ties break by the stated rule and never
by map order (a shuffled-input drill); the planner's offers carry
exactly the top `n`; `--strongest` refuses on an empty ranking; the
agreement refinement reorders only with gold supplied. Mutation
evidence: a disqualified tuple ranked; an offer carrying a tuple
outside the top; a tie broken by iteration order; a clock read in
the derivation; the policy changed without the spec's table changing
(the table is pinned by a drill that parses `ranking.md`).

**D5. Bounds.** No admission change: ranking is policy the supervisor
writes into offers, judged by the existing scope rule; no new verb; the
qualification set rule is unchanged; `eval.Due`'s other acts are
unchanged. The planner lane manifest is unchanged (it takes offers as
before).

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **A ranking exists and is derived.** `ranking.json` orders the
   qualified tuples per capability by the stated table from the
   chain's own facts; no clock, no gold in the projection.
2. **The planner's offers carry the strongest.** `seed offer publish
   --strongest n` and `seed eval act`'s planner-lane offers carry the
   top `n`; an empty ranking refuses rather than widening.
3. **Suspended and disqualified never rank.** Drilled on each path.
4. **Ties are deterministic.** The rule is stated and drilled against
   shuffled input.
5. **Visible.** The doctor names the top tuple per capability; the
   report's planner section carries `strongest`.
6. **Mutation evidence.** Each fails a drill: a disqualified tuple
   ranked; an offer outside the top; a tie by iteration order; a clock
   read; the table changed without the spec.
7. `make check` green with coverage measured cold, three readings
   above the gate; no model identifiers in any committed artifact.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- `offer.published` admits exactly as before; the set rule and the
  eval derivation's mints, disqualifications and spot-checks are
  unchanged; every projection is byte-identical on chains carrying no
  qualification; the planner lane manifest is unchanged.
