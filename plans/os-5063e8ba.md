# Plan: next — a pushed chain citing a stale tip at 200 writers: re-link on the hook's bad_prev, keep the evidence (os-5063e8ba)

The target-scale contention benchmark (os-a00d3f34) ran three
200-writer storms through the `seed-admit` hook on 2026-09-03/04. Two
landed every writer. The second, the one that crossed midnight UTC,
lost one: `writer 157: push rejected by the remote (a policy refusal,
not a race): remote: seed-admit: rule verify: position 558: bad_prev:
prev b692fec4bc38 does not cite tip 2492338b3552`. The hook verified
the pushed chain from genesis and found the writer's own record, at
558, citing the hash of 556 while 557 hashed to something else: the
client committed and pushed a tree carrying a record it had not
linked to. `gitref.Push` classified the hook's refusal as policy (it
is none of the six race shapes), `AppendLoop` surfaced it instead of
re-linking, and the storm's no-lost-updates assertion failed the run,
correctly. Charter III.C row 4 and III.A row 1 (no lost updates under
contention). Tier: standard. Deps: none.

## What the tree actually shows

- **Ruled out by reading.** `Store.Tip` reconciles from the segment
  scan and never trusts HEAD; `segmentNames` sorts, so a chain split
  across `2026-09-03.jsonl` and `2026-09-04.jsonl` scans in order;
  `Append` re-checks `prev` against the reconciled tip, so the
  client's own store held tip b692 when it appended; each writer owns
  its git dir and index; `AppendLoop` materializes into a fresh temp
  dir per attempt; the hook's `materialize` is the client's
  `Materialize` (`git archive | tar -x`); the client's git dir carries
  no `core.checkStat`.
- **Not explained.** How the tree `git add -A` built from that temp
  dir (the client's index persisting across attempts, `write-tree`,
  `commit-tree` on the fetched tip) carried a record the store had
  not reconciled. The one condition unique to the failing run is the
  midnight crossing: the first append after 00:00 opens a new segment
  file, named by the wall clock (`segmentForAppend`), not the record's
  `ts`. The failing chain was not kept: the measurer removes its work
  dir, and `seed perf run` has no way to keep it.
- **The loop cannot answer what it does not classify.** `Push`'s
  comment names six race shapes and says every other rejection is a
  refusal to surface. A `bad_prev` at the position this attempt
  appended is a seventh: either the tip moved in a way the loop did
  not see, or the client's tree is wrong, and re-linking from a fresh
  fetch is the loop's answer to both, bounded by `maxAttempts`.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — the loop re-links on the hook's bad_prev at or beyond its
  own position.** The attempt treats a rejection carrying `bad_prev`
  at a position at or beyond the one `Append` returned as the seventh
  race shape (the storm's refusal was one beyond: the pushed tree
  held a record the store had not reconciled, so the client's own
  record sat one position later than it expected), so `AppendLoop`
  fetches, materializes and re-links as it does for a lost race. A
  `bad_prev` below that position stays a refusal to surface: it is
  the fetched prefix failing at the hook, which the client's own
  verification should have caught first.
- **D2 — the evidence is kept, the commit and the work tree both.**
  On that refusal, before retrying, the attempt keeps under the
  client's state dir at `refused/<commit>/` the tree of the rejected
  commit itself (materialized from the commit with `git archive`, the
  bytes the hook judged) beside the work directory the attempt built
  it from and the hook's message; if the extra record came from the
  client's persistent index, the committed tree carries it and the
  work directory does not, and the difference between the two is the
  diagnosis. The loop's result reports how many re-links the hook's
  refusal cost (`Result.Relinked`), so the next occurrence is a
  directory to read rather than a line in a log. The
  storm surfaces the count: `seed perf run` reports `relinked` in its
  envelope and `perfgate` records it in the reading, with no ceiling
  (a count, not a budget).
- **D3 — `--keep` keeps the work dir.** The measurer gains `Keep`,
  and both `seed perf run` and `cmd/perfgate` take the flag, so a
  failing storm's remote and every writer's state dir survive under a
  named path for the operator; the scale workflow passes `-keep` and
  attaches the directory's refused trees when the run fails (the
  workflow file lands with os-a00d3f34, so the flag reaches it in
  whichever of the two merges second).
- **D4 — the drill plants the shape, not the cause.** A drill builds
  the exact refusal: a client whose temp tree, between `Append` and
  `Commit`, gains a record beyond its reconciled tip (planted through
  a test seam that runs between the two), pushes to a hook-enforced
  bare remote, and is refused `bad_prev` at its own position; the
  drill asserts the loop re-links and lands on the next attempt, the
  refused commit's tree and the work directory are both kept under
  `refused/` with the message, and `Relinked` counts one.
  A second drill crosses an injected midnight (`ledger.WithClock`
  through a new `Client.WithClock` seam used by tests) with two
  writers racing across the segment split and asserts every append
  lands and the pushed chains verify at the hook; if that drill
  reproduces the tree the storm produced, the fix lands in this card
  where the drill points and the plan is amended to name it; if it
  does not, the card lands D1 through D3 and says the cause is open,
  with the drill kept as the regression it is.
- **D5 — bounds.** No change to the hook's verification, the ledger's
  segment naming or the admission rules; the six race shapes stay as
  they are; the per-PR and scale budgets are untouched.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **The loop re-links instead of losing the append.** The planted
   refusal drill lands on the next attempt with `Relinked` one and the
   refused commit's tree and the work directory kept under
   `refused/<commit>/` with the hook's message, the committed tree
   read back through the ledger.
2. **A bad_prev elsewhere still surfaces.** A hook refusal naming a
   position below the appended one is returned as `ErrRemoteRejected`
   with its message, unchanged.
3. **The evidence survives a failed storm.** `seed perf run --keep`
   on a storm with one planted refusal leaves the remote and the
   writers' state dirs under the named path and reports `relinked`.
4. **The midnight drill runs.** Two writers across an injected segment
   split land every append with chains that verify at the hook; the
   drill's outcome (reproduced or not) is stated in the PR and the
   decision log.
5. `make check` green; no model identifiers in any committed
   artifact.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- The six race shapes and every existing gitref, perfgate and perf
  drill pass unchanged; `next/perf/budgets.json` and
  `budgets-scale.json` are byte-identical; the per-PR storm's
  readings are unaffected (a clean storm re-links zero times).
