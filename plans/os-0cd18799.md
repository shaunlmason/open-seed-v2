# Plan (next): the forge says the submission is not mergeable (os-0cd18799)

Backlog card under `docs/next-build-plan.md` §3, filed against charter
§II.4 ("External facts": mirrors of external authorities enter the
ledger as observations, `merge.observed`, `check.observed`), §II.8
(done is a verdict and a reconciliation; the forge's required checks
are the merge gate), §II.11 (the observer records what an external
authority did, the maintenance lane runs unattended and files what it
finds) and §II.13 (the checks gate: CI green plus zero unresolved
threads via forge adapters). Conformance: III.D row 7 (external facts
enter only as observations by governed observers), III.G rows 1 and 2
retained. Tier: standard. Deps: none; Phase 13 is exhausted on the
frontier, so §3 cards are filable, and none may be scheduled ahead of
an open Phase 13 row.

**The gap.** After `submission.made` a contract sits in `review` with
no obligation owed between "the pull request is open" and "the merge
is observed". The verifier judges the acceptance spec's commands in
its own workspace and never reads the forge; a red CI run or an
unresolved review thread is invisible to the verdict. The forge enters
the ledger through one fact only, `merge.observed {merged, pr}`, which
refuses an unmerged pull request by design. The obligations projection
has a closed kind list and none of its kinds says "this submission
needs work", so `seed situation`, the one read every lane orients
from, never tells anyone, and under the one-inbox doctrine nobody
acts. The transition table leaves `review` only through
`merge.observed`, `contract.returned` (citing a fail verdict),
`escalation.raised` and `contract.cancelled`, so the only way a red
pull request gets worked is a full verdict cycle or a human. The
practical consequence is the one this card exists for: an agent opens
the pull request and stops, and a human drives it to green by hand.

## What the tree actually shows

- **`check.observed` is chartered and unimplemented.** The charter
  names it beside `merge.observed` (§II.4) and the protocol register
  lists the `check.*` family (`next/spec/protocol.md`, "external-fact
  observations"); the observer fragment promises "the checks ran and
  came back green or red". No Go file, no capability row, no
  admission rule and no spec page implements it.
- **The forge adapter reads one thing.** `protections.Observer` has
  `Merged(pr) (sha, merged, err)` over GitHub, Forgejo and the
  credential-free snapshot arm (`next/internal/protections/observer.go`,
  plans/os-ad610334.md D4). The protections reconciler WRITES the
  default branch's `required_review_thread_resolution` and
  `required_status_checks` and never reads either back.
- **The return path is verdict-only.** `contract.returned` (`review`
  to `ready`) admits citing `{"verdict": <position>}`, a standing,
  boundary-validated fail on the current submission
  (`next/internal/admit/admit.go`, the `ContractReturnedVerb` arm;
  plans/os-d2497eb7.md); the red-verdict lockout keys on that fail.
  Capability `dispatch` or `operator`.
- **The obligations list is closed** (`next/spec/obligations.md`,
  "The kinds": "The list is closed"); `submission.pending` is owed by
  the verifier lane and `verdict.unmerged` by the operator then the
  observer. Nothing is owed while a pull request is red.
- **The maintenance pass is the unattended runner.** `seed maintain
  run` is one pass in a fixed order (reap, lint, file, rebuild,
  checkpoint) with every effect injected through `maintain.Deps`,
  signed by a key holding `maintenance` and `operator`, wakeless by
  design (`next/spec/maintenance.md`). The worker loop's `Step` is one
  iteration and nothing outside tests and simulation drives it.
- **The submission carries the head but not the pull request.**
  `submission.made`'s packet names `base` as `<merge-base>..<head>`
  (`next/spec/packets.md`); the receipt binds `head` to a full sha
  (`next/spec/verdicts.md`); `merge.observed` takes `pr` from the
  caller. No fold fact names the task pull request.
- **v1 has the gate and not the loop.** The engine's workflow `checks`
  gate queries check runs (REST) and unresolved review threads
  (GraphQL) and is one-shot; the v1 `reject` edge blacklists the
  author (`append_rejected_author`), the opposite of what a red pull
  request needs. The queries are the ones to port; the semantics are
  not.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1. `check.observed` is a fact recorded by the observer, on
  `review` subjects, changing no state.** Strict payload:

  ```json
  {"pr": "<ref>", "head": "<full sha>", "checks": "green" | "red" | "pending",
   "unresolved_threads": <int, optional>, "review": "approved" | "changes_requested" | "none"}
  ```

  `head` is 40 to 64 lowercase hex and must equal the head of the
  current submission window's packet `base` range; an observation of
  another head refuses by name, both shas in the reason, so a stale
  poll can never stand for the submission under review. `checks` is
  the forge's combined state over the head: `green` when every check
  run is completed with a passing conclusion (success, neutral,
  skipped), `red` when any is completed otherwise, `pending` else.
  `unresolved_threads` is the count of unresolved review threads and
  is **absent when the forge cannot say** (Forgejo has no thread
  resolution; `next/spec/forges.md` already names that as
  unexpressible); absent is not zero, and the obligation below treats
  absent as "no thread debt the forge can report" while the forges
  page says so. `review` is the latest review state per reviewer
  reduced to the three literals. The payload carries **no forge
  prose**: no thread bodies, no comment text, no check output, no
  reviewer names. Thread text is the hostile input the dispatcher's
  posture exists for, and the implementer reads it on the forge the
  way `seed message read` works: the reader chooses to look. Admits
  only while the subject folds to `review`, elsewhere exit 3 (the
  illegal-verb-in-state posture, as `verdict.rendered`). Capability
  `observer`, `operator` (the `merge.observed` row). Admission
  additionally requires the observation to **differ** from the
  standing one on the subject in at least one field; the client
  checks the same before drafting (one rule set, advertisement and
  enforcement), so an unchanged poll appends nothing and the
  subject's share of ledger volume is bounded by change, not by
  polling cadence. The fold records the latest admitted observation
  `{position, head, checks, threads, review}` per subject.
  Refused: a payload field for any prose; an observation on a head
  the window does not name; an observation the observer did not
  itself read from the forge or a snapshot.
- **D2. The submission names its pull request.** `submission.made`
  gains an optional sibling field `pr` (the `merge.observed` ref
  grammar: `pr/<n>` or `<n>`) beside `fence`, `packet` and `plan`;
  `seed submission make` gains `--pr`. The fold's `SubmissionFact`
  carries it, and the maintenance pass polls only submissions that
  name one. A submission without `pr` is legal and simply never
  observed, so every existing chain and fixture keeps its meaning.
  Refused: resolving the pull request by branch name inside the
  pass (a forge fact the ledger did not record is not something the
  pass should guess); a required field.
- **D3. One obligation kind, `submission.unmergeable`.** Derived
  when the standing `check.observed` on the current submission window
  has `checks: red`, or `unresolved_threads` above zero, or `review:
  changes_requested`. Not emitted for `pending` alone, and not
  emitted once a fail verdict stands (the return is then owed on the
  verdict's account and `verdict.unmerged` cannot arise). Owed by
  `lane:dispatch`, since the return is queue management, and
  discharged by `contract.returned`; `since` is the observation's
  position; the row carries the observation's `head` so the situation
  read says which revision is red. The closed list in
  `next/spec/obligations.md` grows by exactly this kind with its
  fact-shaped discharger cited, and the drift sweep covers it: the
  advertised discharger must admit for the owed lane at the same
  position. The situation read's marker sweep covers the new row in
  its serialized form, which D1's no-prose rule makes trivially
  true and the sweep keeps true.
- **D4. `contract.returned` cites a red observation as well as a fail
  verdict, and records no lockout.** The payload is exactly one of
  `{"verdict": <position>}` (unchanged: a standing, boundary-validated
  fail on the current submission) or `{"observation": <position>}`: a
  standing `check.observed` on the current submission window that is
  the **latest** observation on the subject and satisfies D3's
  predicate. A later green observation supersedes a red one, so a
  return citing the superseded red refuses by name (the refreshed-view
  rule `merge request` already applies to its derived citation: a
  citation the refreshed view disagrees with refuses rather than
  re-pointing). The red-verdict lockout is untouched: it keys on fail
  verdicts and a return by observation adds no `rejected` fact, so the
  prior submitter is the natural next claimant rather than a
  blacklisted one. The transition table is unchanged: the row
  (`review` to `ready`) exists and this widens its citation rule, not
  its legality. Capability unchanged (`dispatch`, `operator`). The
  fold records which citation the return carried (the contracts
  projection shows it, the report counts returns by kind).
  `merge.requested` additionally refuses while the standing
  observation on the current head satisfies D3's predicate, naming
  the observation: a chain that branch protection would hold anyway
  must not sit `unreconciled` behind it. `merge.overridden` is
  untouched: it overrules a verdict and says nothing about the forge,
  whose protections are the operator's to reconcile.
  Refused: a new transition verb; a lockout on the observation path;
  a return citing an observation that is not the latest.
- **D5. The forge adapter reads checks, threads and review state.**
  `protections.Observer` gains `Checks(pr string) (Observation, error)`
  with `Observation{Head, Checks, UnresolvedThreads *int, Review}`.
  GitHub: `/pulls/{n}` for the head sha, `/commits/{sha}/check-runs`
  for the combined state, the GraphQL `reviewThreads { isResolved }`
  query for the count, `/pulls/{n}/reviews` reduced to the latest
  state per reviewer; the REST and GraphQL queries are ported from the
  v1 engine's `checksGate`, which already runs them against these
  endpoints. Forgejo: `/pulls/{n}` for the head, `/commits/{sha}/status`
  for the combined state, `/pulls/{n}/reviews` for review state, and
  `UnresolvedThreads` nil, named on the forges page. The snapshot arm
  answers the same shape from a file so every drill is
  credential-free; the fake GitHub and Forgejo servers the Phase 13
  item 3 drills use gain these endpoints. `seed check observe
  --forge github|forgejo|snapshot --pr <ref>` drafts D1's fact on both
  postures through the loop verbs' transport (the `merge.go` shape)
  and refuses before signing when the observation is unchanged,
  naming the standing one. The token stays an environment variable
  named by `--token-env`. Refused: an observer that writes to the
  forge; any forge field the fact does not carry; a live-forge drill
  as the only evidence.
- **D6. The maintenance pass observes and returns.** The pass gains
  two steps and runs reap, **observe**, **return**, lint, file,
  rebuild, checkpoint: observations before the lints so the lints and
  the obligations read fresh facts, the return before filing so a
  returned subject is not also filed as a finding, the checkpoint last
  as before. **Observe**: for every `review` subject whose submission
  names a `pr`, call `Deps.Observe(pr)` and append `check.observed`
  when the observation differs from the standing one; a subject whose
  poll fails is reported skipped with the reason, never passed over
  silently. **Return**: for every `submission.unmergeable` row in
  `Deps.Obligations`, append `contract.returned {observation}` unless
  D7's ceiling holds. `Deps.Observe` is injected like every other
  effect; a pass configured with no forge reports the observe step
  skipped with the reason and CI runs that way. Both verbs accept the
  maintenance key's `operator` standing; a key holding `maintenance`
  alone meets `out_of_grant` at the door and the refusal is reported,
  the pass's existing posture. Wakeless as before: a forge webhook is
  an advisory wake adapter (§II.9) and is out of this card's scope,
  named in the decision log; the pass on its schedule is the
  correctness path. Refused: a poll inside the worker loop; a
  scheduler inside the verb; a retry on a refused act.
- **D7. A return ceiling, then an escalation.** `Deps.ReturnCeiling`
  (a declared threshold in the `expiry_after` shape, CLI
  `--return-ceiling`, default 3) bounds the number of admitted
  `contract.returned {observation}` on one subject. At the ceiling
  the pass appends `escalation.raised` on the subject (`review` to
  `blocked`, a legal row; the maintenance key holds `operator`) with a
  packet naming the standing observation's position and head, the
  count, and one decision: raise the ceiling, cancel, or merge by the
  operator's own hand. This is §II.13's "max revisions" on the
  contract loop, and it is what stops a red pull request from cycling
  forever unattended. Budget reservations bound spend per window
  exactly as they do today, so the ceiling bounds rounds, not money.
  Refused: a ceiling read from a wall clock; an escalation that
  carries thread text.
- **D8. The lane conventions say what a red return means.** The
  implementer fragment gains one paragraph: a returned contract whose
  packet cites a `check.observed` is yours to make mergeable; every
  unresolved thread gets a push or a reply on the forge, every red
  check a fix or a named cause in the packet; the receipt is
  regenerated at the last commit before resubmission, because every
  push invalidates the one bound to the earlier head; you resubmit
  only when your own forge read says green, naming the same `pr`. The
  maintenance fragment gains one sentence (observe, return, escalate
  at the ceiling). The observer fragment is unchanged: it already
  promised the checks. The handbook's lanes section gains the observe
  verb beside `merge observe`. Preferring the prior submitter's tuple
  for the re-offer is supervisor ranking policy (Phase 13 item 7) and
  is filed as a follow-up card in this task's PR, not built here.
  Refused: a bare "fix CI" instruction with no act behind it.
- **D9. Protocol `seed/8`.** A new verb a `seed/7` validator's
  unknown-verb arm would fail on, and a widened `contract.returned`
  payload a `seed/7` validator would refuse, so the two judge a
  `seed/8` record differently: hence the bump, in the register's
  established shape. At `seed/7` positions `check.observed` stays
  unknown-and-refused under a `seed/8` validator and a return cites a
  verdict only. `submission.made`'s optional `pr` is additive catalog
  growth active from `seed/8`.
- **D10. Bounds.** No change to `next/spec/transitions.json` or
  `internal/transition/table.json`; no change to the verdict rule, the
  lockout, the override, or the verifier's workspace; nothing outside
  `next/**` except the work-product files below. NOT `.seed/**`, NOT
  `scripts/**`, NOT `.github/workflows/**`.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **The fact admits and binds.** `check.observed` admits on a `review`
   subject from an `observer` key with a head equal to the submission's,
   refuses on any other state (exit 3), refuses a foreign head naming
   both shas, refuses an unchanged observation naming the standing
   one, and refuses any payload field beyond D1's.
2. **The obligation stands and discharges.** `seed situation` for the
   dispatch lane shows `submission.unmergeable` after a red, a
   threads-above-zero, or a changes-requested observation; not after
   `pending` alone; not after a fail verdict; and the drift sweep
   admits `contract.returned` for the owed lane at every position the
   row stands.
3. **The return by observation.** `contract.returned {observation}`
   admits citing the latest red observation, refuses a superseded one
   and a green one by name, records no lockout, and the prior
   submitter reclaims the re-offered subject; `merge.requested`
   refuses while the standing observation is red and admits once a
   green one supersedes it.
4. **The adapter reads both forges.** `Observer.Checks` returns the
   same shape from the fake GitHub, the fake Forgejo and a snapshot;
   Forgejo's thread count is nil and the forges page says so.
5. **The pass drives the loop unattended.** In the modes fixture, one
   `seed maintain run` over a red snapshot appends the observation and
   the return, a second over a green snapshot after resubmission
   appends the green observation and returns nothing, and the chain
   completes to `done` through the unchanged verdict and merge chain;
   with no forge configured the observe step reports skipped with a
   reason; a `maintenance`-only key sees both acts refused
   `out_of_grant` and reported.
6. **The ceiling escalates.** The fourth red return on one subject
   under the default ceiling is an `escalation.raised` carrying the
   observation's position, the head, the count and one decision, and
   nothing on the subject moves until it is answered.
7. **No prose crosses.** The injection marker planted in every forge
   field the fakes serve (thread bodies, review bodies, check output,
   reviewer names) appears in no ledger payload, no obligation row and
   no situation read.
8. **The version bump.** A `seed/7` validator refuses a chain carrying
   `check.observed` at the first `seed/8` record by version; at `seed/7`
   positions the verb is unknown-and-refused under `seed/8`.
9. **Mutation evidence.** Each fails a drill: an observation admitted
   on a foreign head; a return citing a superseded observation; a
   lockout recorded on the observation path; an unchanged observation
   admitted; a thread body carried in the payload; the observe step
   skipped silently; a ceiling read from the clock; the transition
   table edited.
10. `make check` green with coverage measured cold, three readings
    above the gate; the generated docs drift-free; no model
    identifiers in any committed artifact.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- Every existing chain and fixture folds with the same meaning
  (`pr` absent, returns by verdict only); the verdict rule, the
  red-verdict lockout, `merge.overridden` and the full reconciliation
  chain admit exactly as before; `merge.observed` and the Forgejo
  and GitHub protections drills are unchanged; the shipped lanes
  validate clean; the compromised-actor drill and the release gate
  are unchanged.
