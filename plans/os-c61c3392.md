# Plan: next Phase 7.1 — offers (os-c61c3392)

Implements `docs/next-build-plan.md` Phase 7 item 1: `offer.published`,
eligibility-scoped and expiring; workers pull and claim; and the
wakeless poll-only drill proving wake is advisory. Design authority:
SEED-NEXT.md §II.9 ("Offers, not assignments; pull with advisory wake
(normative)": the supervisor publishes offers, workers pull and claim,
the claim settles at admission — first valid claim wins, exactly like
any claim; wake is advisory transport, never the grant itself; there
is no assignment to orphan, only offers that get claimed or expire)
and charter III.H rows 1–2 (offers-and-claims proven by a wakeless
poll-only CI run; offers expire; duplicate scheduling impossible
because exclusivity settles at admission). Verb-catalog growth is
additive, which `next/spec/protocol.md` states does not bump the
protocol version. Budget-reservation scoping lands with 7.2, the wake
channel and adapters with 7.3, runtime tuples with Phase 10: the offer
schema stays honestly minimal rather than reserving dead fields.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — capability.** New `supervise` capability (`CapSupervise`),
  the supervisor lane the charter names; `offer.published` accepts
  {supervise, operator}, the standard operator fallback (dispatch/
  claim pattern). No disjointness constraints: an offer grants
  nothing — the claim settles at admission like any claim — so
  supervisor standing carries no authoring or judging authority the
  capability audit must separate.
- **D2 — payload and admission window.** Strict payload
  `{"eligibility": {"capabilities": [...], "tiers": [...]},
  "expires": "<RFC3339>"}` (DisallowUnknownFields; `eligibility`
  required, its arrays optional — empty scopes mean any active
  worker, any tier; `expires` required). Admitted only while the
  subject folds to `ready` (a re-readied subject — released, reaped,
  returned — is offerable again by a fresh publication; multiple
  live offers on one subject are legal). Born-dead offers refuse deterministically: `expires`
  must parse RFC3339 and lie strictly after the event's own `ts` —
  admission never reads a wall clock. No position throttle in v0:
  publishing is supervise/operator-gated, so volume is a governance
  matter, unlike open-lane milestones.
- **D3 — the laundering shape, applied where offers are consumed.**
  The tolerant fold records any well-shaped `offer.published` as an
  OfferFact (raw pushes included, like verdicts and overrides); the
  consuming surface — `offer list` — validates the offer signer's
  {supervise, operator} standing position-accurately at the offer's
  admission position (the VerifySeals/VerifyOverrides replay
  pattern), so a raw-pushed foreign offer is inert: never listed,
  never authority. Offers never gate claims in either direction — a
  claim on a subject with no offer, or only consumed, expired, or
  foreign offers, admits exactly as today. No reconcile changes: offers do
  not participate in done-ness.
- **D4 — claimed-or-expire liveness, fully derived, no events.**
  Liveness = subject currently `ready` AND `expires` after now AND
  no `claim.taken` admitted on the subject after the offer's
  position: a claim consumes every offer at or before it, so a taken
  offer never zombies back when the subject re-readies (release,
  park, reap, return) — re-offering re-readied work is a fresh
  publication carrying current supervisor intent, never a stale one
  (the charter's "claimed or expire" terminal outcomes). The fold
  records the subject's last `claim.taken` position as the
  consumption boundary. `offer list` takes `--now <RFC3339>` (tests
  inject it; default wall clock). No `offer.expired` or
  `offer.withdrawn` verbs in v0: expiry and consumption need no
  writer. Facts persist in the fold; nothing is erased.
- **D5 — versions.** Catalog gains the `offer.*` namespace
  (additive, no protocol bump). Fold shape change bumps contracts
  projection "8"→"9" (`offers` serialized with omitempty so
  offer-free chains keep byte-identical views), report "5"→"6",
  cache generation 7→8 with `cacheVersion` "8"→"9". New
  `next/spec/offers.md` documents the scheduling model; the
  `next/spec/projections.md` named-extension-points sentence updates
  to reflect offers landing.
- **D6 — CLI.** `seed offer publish --ledger --key --subject
  --expires [--capability c]... [--tier t]...` (shapes the payload,
  appends through the same validated path as every append) and
  `seed offer list --ledger --actor <fp> [--now]` (read-only
  envelope: live offers whose eligibility the actor meets). Refusals
  reuse the existing admission-refusal envelope exits; no new exit
  codes, `next/spec/envelope.md` untouched.

## Steps

1. **Catalog and spec.** Add `offer.*` — `published` — to the
   `next/spec/protocol.md` catalog (citing the additive-growth
   clause). Write `next/spec/offers.md`: the §II.9 scheduling model,
   payload schema, admission window, eligibility semantics, expiry
   as derived liveness, the advisory-wake posture (poll-only loses
   only latency), and the foreign-offer inertness note. Update the
   `next/spec/projections.md` extension-points sentence and the
   `next/spec/actors.md` capability table (new `supervise` row; the
   `offer.published` accepted-capabilities row).
2. **Keyring.** `CapSupervise` const; `AcceptedCapabilities` row for
   `offer.published` = {supervise, operator}; vocabulary and
   completeness test rows; actors.md pin stays byte-accurate.
3. **Fold.** `OfferFact{Pos, Signer, Capabilities, Tiers, Expires}`
   on SubjectState (`offers`, omitempty); the fold appends one per
   well-shaped admitted or raw `offer.published`, and records the
   subject's last `claim.taken` position (the D4 consumption
   boundary, serialized in the contracts "9" views); malformed
   payloads fold to nothing, matching the tolerant posture.
4. **Admission.** New named `offer` rule in `Default()` after the
   verdict rule: verb-gated, strict-decodes the payload, refuses
   unknown fields, missing/empty `expires` or malformed RFC3339,
   `expires` not strictly after the event `ts`, and any subject not
   currently folding to `ready` (InvalidTransitionError posture).
   Capability rides the existing grant rule; Check previews it
   cooperatively like every rule.
5. **CLI.** `next/cmd/seed/offer.go`: `publish` and `list` per D6.
   List filters: state `ready`, unexpired at `--now`, unconsumed
   (no later claim, per D4), eligibility met (offer capabilities ⊆
   the actor's current active grants; subject tier ∈ offer tiers
   when scoped), signer boundary-valid at the offer's position per
   D3.
6. **Drills** (`offer_cli_test.go` + package tests), each naming its
   conformance row: the **wakeless poll-only run** — file → specify
   → publish → worker polls `offer list`, sees the offer with no
   wake channel in existence → claim → submit → verdict pass →
   merge.requested/observed → done (III.H row 1); the **duplicate-
   scheduling race** — two eligible workers see one offer, both take
   `claim.taken`, exactly one admits, the loser gets the structured
   contention refusal (III.H row 2); **expiry** — an expired offer
   stops listing (`--now` injected) and the subject remains
   claimable ("no assignments to orphan"); the **re-ready
   regression** — after `claim.taken` then `claim.released`, the
   original unexpired offer no longer lists (consumed by the claim)
   while a fresh publication on the re-readied subject does list,
   and the same holds behind park/reap/return; **foreign-offer
   inertness** — a raw-pushed offer by a plain enrolled key folds
   but never lists; **born-dead refusal**; **not-ready refusals**
   (backlog, in_progress, review, done) and the re-readied re-offer
   admitting; eligibility filtering (capability mismatch, tier
   mismatch, empty scopes matching all).
7. **Projections.** Contracts "9" with an offer-bearing chain view
   fixture added and offer-free views byte-identical; report "6";
   cache generation 8 / `cacheVersion` "9"; user_version test
   updated.
8. **Docs.** `next/docs/progress.md`: 7.1 ledger row and frontier
   updated. `next/docs/decisions.md`: one line per D1–D6.
   `memory/LEARNINGS.md` only if implementation surfaces a durable
   insight; no speculative entry.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

- `offer.published` admits on a `ready` subject from a
  supervise-granted key and from an operator; refuses out-of-grant
  for a plain worker, in every non-ready state, on unknown payload
  fields, and born-dead (expires ≤ event ts) — each with the named
  structured refusal.
- The wakeless poll-only drill reaches `done` with no wake channel,
  and the race drill admits exactly one of two claims with the
  structured contention refusal for the loser; both tests carry
  `// conformance: III.H` comments.
- `offer list` shows a live eligible offer to a matching worker and
  hides: expired offers, offers consumed by a later claim (the
  re-ready regression: released, parked, reaped, or returned work
  never resurrects its taken offer, while a fresh publication
  lists), tier/capability mismatches, offers on non-ready subjects,
  and raw-pushed foreign offers (position-accurate boundary), while
  a claim on a subject with only consumed, expired, or foreign
  offers still admits.
- Contracts "9" golden views include an offer-bearing chain;
  offer-free views are byte-identical to "8"'s bodies; report "6"
  and cache generation 8 land with their tests.

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- `make check` (with `check-next`) green; coverage stays ≥90%.
- No existing exit code, refusal string, or envelope shape changes;
  every 6.x drill (verdict, seal, lockout, override, reconcile)
  passes unmodified.
- No v1 surface changes; the task PR never touches `plans/**`.
