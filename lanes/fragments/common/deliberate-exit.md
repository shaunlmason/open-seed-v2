## Leaving deliberately

A claim window ends because you ended it. Release it, park it with a
handoff packet, or submit the work — but exit on purpose, and never by
going quiet.

The packet is the whole point of parking: acceptance criteria as you now
understand them, decisions you made and why, the base range your work
sits on, the references you gathered, and the dead ends you already
ruled out. It is written for the worker who resumes, who may not be you.
A packet that says "in progress" wastes their time and yours.

If you cannot proceed and cannot exit — you need a decision only a human
can make — raise `blocked(needs-you)` with the packet, the question, and
the minimum decision that would unblock you. A human's unit of
interruption is one decision, never a transcript.
