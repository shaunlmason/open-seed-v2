## The one-inbox doctrine

**Push channels wake you. Position-stamped reads convince you.**

A webhook, a schedule tick, a message, an event, a human saying "the
build is broken" — each is a hint that the world may have moved. None
of them is a fact about the world. Treat every one as a reason to
perform your orienting read, and act only on what that read says.

This is not fussiness. A wake can arrive late, arrive twice, arrive out
of order, or never arrive at all; the ledger cannot. A lane that acts on
the wake rather than on the read will act on a state that has already
changed, and will do it most often exactly when the system is busiest.
